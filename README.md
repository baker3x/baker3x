# Chase Baker

Co-founder and CTO at Unplugged AI. I build and evaluate offline language-model systems, with a focus on retrieval quality, grounded answers, and failure analysis.

## Selected work

- **[Cosine matcher false-positive study](https://github.com/Unplugged-AI-org/unplugged-evals/blob/main/studies/cosine_false_positive_study.md)** — I designed and ran this study, widening procedure-step matching to 92 steps across 17 units. At the selected threshold, nine cross-unit false positives remained. I analyzed the errors and kept automated step completion out of the pilot UI. This was an exploratory candidate study.
- **[Unplugged AI evaluation brief](https://github.com/Unplugged-AI-org/unplugged-evals)** — A public account of a 61-case curated procedure-identification evaluation, including its primarily synthetic test set, recorded results, methodology, and limits. I built the underlying systems and evaluation work at Unplugged AI.
- **[Steel City SLM workshop](https://github.com/baker3x/steel-city-workshop)** — A small-model fine-tuning workshop with an illustrative comparison and held-out format check. It is a teaching demonstration; the full Colab run still needs verification.

## Current focus

I am interested in empirical methods for finding consequential model and evaluator failures, especially when retrieval, generation, and safeguards interact in local systems.
